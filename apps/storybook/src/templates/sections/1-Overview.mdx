## When to use

Use it for a group of short, parallel topics where readers typically open one or two (FAQs, hazards, coverage details).
For a single block of content use `ds-accordion`; for a standalone list of links or actions use `ds-list` on its own.

<StoryHeading of={SectionStories.SummarySection}></StoryHeading>

A title on the left and a colored box on the right that summarizes an article in a checkmark list.

<CanvasWithCodePen of={SectionStories.SummarySection} sourceState="hidden" />

## When to use

Use it at the top of the content of a blog article or long page to give readers the key points before they read on.
Pick the box color with the color control, matching the color of the stage. For a list of interactive rows use
`ds-list`; for content readers open on demand use `ds-accordion`.

<StoryHeading of={SectionStories.TeaserCarouselSection}></StoryHeading>

A title on the left and a carousel of teasers on the right. Each teaser has an image, a heading, a short text and one
link. The carousel shows one teaser on mobile, two from the tablet and three from the desktop breakpoint.

<CanvasWithCodePen of={SectionStories.TeaserCarouselSection} sourceState="hidden" />

## When to use

Use it to promote a handful of related pages, each with an image and a short text, where a row would not fit them all.
Keep the text short (about 200 characters) and give every teaser exactly one link. For a strip of icon tiles that are
links themselves use `ds-carousel` with `navigation` items; for a single highlighted block use `ds-card`.
